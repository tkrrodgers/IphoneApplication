# Logsperity Privacy Policy

*Draft for Prosperity Simplified to review. Last updated: October 31, 2026.*

Logsperity is made by Prosperity Simplified. This policy covers the Logsperity iPhone app.

## The short version

Your logs stay on your iPhone. Prosperity Simplified does not collect, receive, store or sell them.

## What the app stores, and where

- **Your entries** (prospects, closings, income, expenses, mileage, activity time, sources and your business plan)
  and **your account details** (name, company, profession, contact details, reminder time) are saved only on your
  iPhone, in the app's private storage, protected by your device passcode.
- The app has **no account or sign-in** and **no server**. Nothing you enter is sent to Prosperity Simplified.
- If you delete the app, or use More → Erase all data, your entries are deleted from your iPhone.
- More → Export my data creates a file you can share. Where it goes is up to you.

## Microphone and speech

When you tap the microphone in Ask HERB, the app listens until you stop and turns your speech into text. Apple's
speech recognition runs on your iPhone when the device supports it; otherwise Apple may process the audio under
Apple's privacy policy. Logsperity does not record or keep audio.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your iPhone. No information is sent
anywhere to do this.

## Analytics, ads and tracking

Logsperity has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## TestFlight pilot

If you test the app through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with
Prosperity Simplified, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms.

## Children

Logsperity is a business app and isn't directed at children under 13.

## Changes and contact

We'll update this page if the app's handling of data changes (for example, when optional iCloud sync is added).
Questions: contact Prosperity Simplified at the support address listed on the App Store page.
